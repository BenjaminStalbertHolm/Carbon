Section: §1 (Platform)

Options you see: A: ad-hoc only, as now / B: Developer ID signed and notarized (needs an Apple developer account and credentials kept outside the repo) / C: something else

## QUESTION-4
Section: §11.3 (`paper_out.wav`)
Context: The spec says `paper_out` uses a "reversed envelope (fast rise, slow fall)" of the same noise-times-Hann recipe as `paper_in`. A Hann window is symmetric, so reversing it changes nothing.
Question: Which envelope should `paper_out` use?
Options you see: A: fast 50 ms rise, then a cosine-squared slow fall to 600 ms (current placeholder, `tools/gen_audio.py` `TODO(QUESTION-4)`) / B: identical to `paper_in` (Hann) / C: something else

## QUESTION-5
Section: §8.10 (Clock) with §13.4 (End of shift)
Context: §8.10 gives the clock as 09:00 + 8 h × k/n after the k-th of n tasks is sent. For the last task of a day (k = n) that is 17:00. §8.10 and §13.4 then set the clock to 16:58 when the End of Shift memo arrives. Days 1 to 3 have 3 tasks and Day 5 has 2.
Question: What should the clock show when the last task of a day is sent?
Options you see: A: 17:00 at that moment, then 16:58 when the End of Shift memo arrives (literal reading) / B: 16:58 at that moment, with no 17:00 step / C: something else

## QUESTION-6
Section: §13.4 (End of shift) with §14.9 and §14.10 (Days 3 and 4)
Context: §13.4 says the End of Shift memo arrives 5.0 s after the last task completes. §14.9 and §14.10 give a different trigger for Days 3 and 4: whichever comes first of 5.0 s after ghost line 1 finishes typing, or 120 s after the ghost memo arrives (Day 3) or after S-4 is sent (Day 4).
Question: Do the Day 3 and Day 4 triggers replace §13.4 for those days?
Options you see: A: yes, the day-specific trigger replaces §13.4 on Days 3 and 4 / B: no, §13.4 applies on every day and the ghost-line trigger is ignored / C: something else
